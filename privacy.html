Privacy Policy

Dinares: Islamic Quiz Game

Effective November 10, 2026

Overview

Dinares: Islamic Quiz Game (“Dinares”, “we”, “us”) is developed by EG Digital. This policy explains what happens to information when you use the app.

The short version: we do not collect personal data. Dinares has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and no server of ours receives anything about you. Your progress is stored on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect your name, e-mail, location, contacts, usage analytics, crash reports or advertising identifiers, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

What exists, and where it lives:

  • Game progress — scores, levels, badges, trails and settings are stored locally on your device (including a small shared copy used by the Home Screen widget). Deleting the app deletes them.
  • Game Center — if you are signed in to Game Center, the app sends your scores to Apple's Game Center leaderboards. Your Game Center nickname and scores are handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive your Apple Account details. You can turn Game Center off in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Purchases — subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed entirely by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We never see or store your payment details.
  • Reminders — optional daily reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. No push token is created or sent to us.

How we use information

We hold no personal information, so there is nothing for us to use, share or sell. We do not show advertising and do not build profiles.

Third-party services

Dinares bundles no third-party SDKs — no analytics, attribution, crash reporting or advertising. The only external services are Apple's own: the App Store / StoreKit for purchases and Game Center for leaderboards, both governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Dinares includes a Kids mode with age-appropriate questions and stories. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children. The kids profile never sends scores to Game Center. Purchases require the Apple Account holder's approval under Apple's own parental controls (Ask to Buy / Screen Time).

Data retention and your rights

Laws such as the GDPR and the LGPD give you rights over personal data a company holds about you — access, correction, deletion and complaint to a supervisory authority. Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing on our side to access or delete. Your progress is removed when you delete the app; Game Center data is managed in your Apple Account.

Questions? Write to sos@egdigital.com and we will reply within 30 days.

Security

The safest data is data that is never collected. Your on-device progress is protected by iOS itself; Game Center and purchases are protected by your Apple Account.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the “Effective” date above. Continued use of Dinares after an update means you accept the updated policy.
