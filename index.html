Dinares: Islamic Quiz Game

Climb the ladder to one million dinares

Dinares is an Islamic quiz game. Answer questions about the Quran, the Prophets, the Seerah, Hadith, the Pillars of Islam and Islamic history, and climb a ladder of prizes all the way to one million dinares.

Every question comes with its reference — a Quran surah and ayah, or a hadith from Sahih al-Bukhari or Sahih Muslim — so you learn while you play.

  • 13 categories, from Juz Amma and Makkan surahs to the Companions and the Holy Places
  • Beginner to Sheikh levels, a Study mode, a Timed mode and a Kids mode
  • Learning trails, symbol cards and a Game Center leaderboard
  • Available in English, Arabic, Indonesian, Malay, Turkish, French and Portuguese

No account. No ads. Your progress stays on your device.
