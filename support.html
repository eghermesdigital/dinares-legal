Contact us

Write to sos@egdigital.com. We usually reply within two business days. Please include your device model, iOS version and app version.

Frequently asked questions

I found a question or reference that looks wrong.
Send us the question text and the reference shown under the answer. Every question cites a Quran ayah or a hadith collection and number, and we review every report.

How do I restore my purchase?
Open the Dinares Premium screen in the app and tap Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?
On your iPhone or iPad open Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions › Dinares › Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date to avoid the next charge. You keep access until the end of the paid period.

How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

The leaderboard does not load.
Make sure you are signed in to Game Center in iOS Settings › Game Center and connected to the internet. The kids profile never appears on leaderboards.

Do I need an account?
No. Dinares has no account and no login. Your progress is stored on your device.

Can I change the language?
Dinares follows your device language (English, Arabic, Indonesian, Malay, Turkish, French or Portuguese). You can also pick a language for the app only in iOS Settings › Dinares › Language.
